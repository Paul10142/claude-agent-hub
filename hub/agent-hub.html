The board from your Mac: each Claude session from the last 24 hours that isn't archived, in the same columns. In the Claude app on your Mac it's live, updating every 30 seconds or when you tap Refresh. Anywhere else it shows the last saved copy.

“Open in Claude” opens the session's chat in the Claude app on your Mac. On a phone it opens the chat on claude.ai instead, for sessions with Remote Control on.

Sessions run on your Mac, so while it sleeps they pause, the chats can't take new messages, and this page keeps its last copy.
